# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Moved the launcher into the existing composer toolbar as a compact labeled button, instead of a screen-fixed floating button or an extra dock row. It follows the window layout and never covers the conversation.
- Kept the icon-only form on narrow windows.
- Rebuilt `client.js`.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release: Bode, Nyquist and root-locus plots with step-by-step calculations.
- Symbolic `K` support (numerator or denominator).
- Pure delay support: exact in Bode/Nyquist, second-order Padé in root locus.
- Zoom/pan and offline operation.
